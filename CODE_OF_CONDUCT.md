# Code of conduct

Signpost exists so that teams can tell each other what is changing without
anyone being caught out. That only works if it is safe to post an
inconvenient date, to ask an obvious question, and to say "this broke us".

## What we expect

- **Assume the other team is doing their best.** A late migration or a missed
  deprecation is usually a capacity problem, not a character problem.
- **Argue with the plan, not the person.** "This date doesn't give us enough
  time, here's why" is welcome. "This is a stupid way to run a platform" is
  not.
- **Be concrete.** If something here is wrong, say what is wrong and what it
  should say instead.
- **Accept that people are wrong sometimes, including you.** Correcting a
  published date quickly matters more than being right the first time.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment of any kind; personal or political attacks; sexualised language or
imagery; insults, slurs or demeaning comments; publishing someone's private
information without permission; and sustained disruption of discussions or
review.

This applies to everything in and around this repository: issues, pull
requests, commit messages, review comments and the content published through
them.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks these rules, report it to
the maintainers listed in `.github/CODEOWNERS`. If the problem involves a
maintainer, report it to your organization's usual channel for this — in most
companies that is HR or a people partner, and going there directly is always a
legitimate choice.

Reports are handled privately. We will not share a reporter's identity with
the person reported without asking first.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions that break these rules, and
may temporarily or permanently block anyone who keeps doing so. We will
explain the reason for any action we take, and a first response is usually a
private word rather than a public one.

## For forks

This is a deliberately short code of conduct, written for the kind of
repository Signpost is: internal, and read mostly by colleagues. If your
project would rather adopt a standard, widely recognised text, replace this
file with the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) and
fill in its contact details.
